# Outreach Pipeline: Spec Work + Direct Outreach

The idea: for roles worth the extra effort, don't just apply. Make a small, honest proof
piece in the company's world, tailor the resume, and send both to a real person on the
hiring side.

## The pipeline (each role moves through these stages)

| Stage | What happens | Who | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Pick | Role scores B or better, or it's a reach worth the effort | Ben + Claude | Role is on the Applications or Opportunities tab |
| 2. Brief | Claude writes the spec-piece assignment from the job posting | Claude | Brief approved by Ben |
| 3. Find the person | Ben checks LinkedIn (logged in); Claude assesses the profiles Ben pastes | Ben + Claude | One hiring-side contact plus one recruiter picked |
| 4. Make | Ben builds the spec piece, with Claude helping on copy, prompts and structure | Ben | Piece is reviewed and labeled as spec work |
| 5. Tailor | Claude reorders resume bullets and the summary for the role; Ben exports from Claude Design | Claude + Ben | PDF is ready |
| 6. Send | A short LinkedIn note or email with the piece and the resume | Ben | Sent; logged in the tracker |
| 7. Follow up | One nudge 7-10 days later, then stop | Ben | Logged |

**Rules**
- Timebox spec work to 6-8 hours. It's proof, not free labor.
- Label every piece: "Spec concept by Ben Santiago. Not produced for or approved by [Company]." Use only public brand cues.
- Don't invent stats, customer quotes, or claims about the product (this matters most at Circle, which is a regulated fintech).
- Send nothing until Ben has reviewed it.

## Assignment 1: Engine, Creative Director (Growth). Priority: do this first.

**Why this piece:** the role is to "design the end-to-end creative pipeline: workflows, prompt
libraries, components, guardrails, asset databases" and to treat creative as a growth lever
(hook rate, hold rate, testing velocity). So show them a working mini version.

**Deliverable: "A one-week AI creative sprint for Engine"**
1. **Workflow map (1 page).** Brief → brand rules and prompt library → generation
   (Magnific for imagery, Claude Design for layout) → QA guardrails → variants by hook and
   audience → test plan → learnings feed the next brief. Mark which steps are AI, which are
   human, and where review happens.
2. **Sample ad set (9 statics).** One concept territory × 3 hooks × 3 formats (1:1, 4:5, 9:16).
   - Suggested territory: group travel or team offsites ("Get the whole team there without the spreadsheet"). Pick one from Engine's public site.
   - Each hook targets a different buyer: travel manager, finance or ops lead, and team lead.
3. **Test plan (half a page).** What you'd test first, what you'd measure (hook rate, hold rate,
   click-through rate, cost per lead), and what result would change the next round.
4. **Optional:** a 2-minute Loom walking through the system. This is the strongest proof
   that the process is yours.

**Proof points to mention:** your Point of Rental design system, the 50-70 carousels per session,
your guardrails, and IC26 for the events side of the role.
**Be honest about:** you haven't run a performance-creative team at scale. Frame the test plan
as how you'd learn quickly, not as past results.
**Target dates:** brief and contact by Nov 17, piece by Nov 20, send Nov 21. That arrives
before the Nov 23 follow-up, and the piece then is the follow-up.

## Assignment 2: Circle, Principal, Creative Producer. Lighter version, since it's a reach.

**Why this piece:** the role is to "recommend the right approach for ideas" across formats and to be
the team's AI-production expert. So show your production judgment, not just output.

**Deliverable: "How I'd produce one Circle campaign"**
1. **Production plan (1-2 pages)** for one hypothetical idea (for example, "money that moves at
   internet speed" for USDC payments):
   - each asset (hero film, social cutdowns, event or experiential, statics) with a recommended mode: live action, AI, or hybrid, and why
   - tool stack, a rough timeline, budget tiers (lean, standard, hero) and the partner types to hire
   - compliance notes for a regulated fintech
2. **6-frame storyboard or 15-second AI animatic** for the hero piece, to show your craft.
3. **A short "where AI helps / where it doesn't" note.** The posting asks for exactly this judgment.

**Be honest about:** it asks for 10+ years running film and immersive productions. Lead with judgment
and speed, not scale.
**Target dates:** piece by Nov 22; send it as your Nov 23 follow-up.

## Finding the right person (needs your logged-in LinkedIn)

I can't see LinkedIn people pages, hiring teams or post activity without a login, and
public web search didn't reliably name Engine's or Circle's creative leaders. So:

1. Open the job post while logged in. If there's a **"Meet the hiring team"** box, that person is
   your first contact.
2. On the company's LinkedIn page, go to **People** and search these keywords:
   - **Engine:** "growth", "creative", "brand", "head of marketing". The CD role sits in Growth and works with the Brand team.
   - **Circle:** "creative", "brand", "production", "design". The role supports the "Creative & Design" team leadership.
3. For each candidate, check the **Activity** tab. A post or comment in the last 30 days is a good
   sign they'll read messages.
4. Paste me 2-4 profile URLs, or just the names and titles, and I'll pick the best fit and draft
   the note. One is the person the role likely reports to; one is the recruiter.
