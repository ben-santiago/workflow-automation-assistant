# AI Creative Leadership Job Search: Research Agent Brief

Ben's brief, saved 2026-11-05. It is the source of truth for every daily run.

## Objective
Continuously discover, verify, evaluate and rank newly posted creative leadership
roles in the US that are **high-paying and fully remote**. They should fit a candidate
who combines creative direction, B2B SaaS marketing, multidisciplinary production
and AI-powered creative automation. Prioritize realistic, competitive fits over
the highest-paying executive roles.

## 1. Candidate
- Fort Worth, TX. Target base: $130k-$160k+. Must have a published base range whose
  maximum is at least $125k. Full-time, permanent. Fully remote, explicitly Texas-eligible.
- Creative Lead at Point of Rental Software (B2B SaaS for equipment rental, events and AV).
- Leads multidisciplinary work: brand, digital, social, video, photo, print, web,
  trade shows, integrated campaigns.
- Builds scalable creative processes, production workflows and PM systems.
- Strong in B2B software marketing, product storytelling, demand gen and sales enablement.
- Freelance agency work: brand development, web design, content production, creative strategy.
- Uses Claude and other generative AI to build repeatable creative systems
  and automate brand content workflows.
- Strategic leader and hands-on practitioner.
- **Value proposition: a creative leader who builds systems, not just assets.**
  Look for orgs that need to scale output with brand consistency, speed and less manual work.

**Secondary:**

## 3. Employers
Industries, in order of preference:
1. B2B SaaS and enterprise tech
2. AI-native software and automation platforms
3. Tech-enabled consumer brands
4. Creative tech and design platforms
5. Media, entertainment and creator economy
6. Premium lifestyle, home, travel and consumer brands

Give extra weight to verified workplace awards: Fortune Best Companies, Great Place To
Work, Forbes Best Employers and Best Startup Employers, Built In Best Places, Comparably.
Verify the specific award and year.

## 4. Mandatory filters
**Required:**
- Open and accepting applications
- Full-time, permanent
- Remote in the US, Texas-eligible, with no conflicting geo restrictions
- Published base range with a maximum of at least $125k
- Real creative, brand, production or systems responsibility
- An individual posting with a verifiable employer and application link

**Exclude:**
- Hybrid or office roles outside Fort Worth/Dallas
- Remote roles whose state lists exclude TX
- Contract, freelance or temp roles
- No published base salary, or $125k reached only through bonus or equity
- Pure UX or product design
- Pure copywriting, unless it carries substantial creative direction
- Roles needing far more seniority than the candidate has
- Closed or unverifiable postings

Roles with a ceiling above $125k but a low floor are eligible, but rank them lower.

## 5. Freshness and sources
- Search daily: the last 24-72 hours first, then 7-14 days.
- Sources: LinkedIn, employer career pages, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday,
  Built In, Wellfound and other credible boards.
- Verify every role on the official posting.
- Give the exact individual LinkedIn job URL, or mark it unavailable and give the official URL.
- Never invent job IDs, dates, salaries, rankings or eligibility.

## 6. Scoring (0-100)

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Alignment with creative leadership experience | 25 |
| B2B SaaS, tech or relevant industry | 20 |
| AI workflows, automation and creative systems opportunity | 20 |
| Salary alignment | 15 |
| Realistic seniority and qualifications | 10 |
| Employer reputation and stability | 10 |

**Priority bands:**
- **A+ (90-100):** apply now
- **A (80-89):** recommend applying
- **B (70-79):** worth considering, with compromises
- **C (under 70):** don't notify unless unusually compelling

Don't inflate scores for prestige. State gaps clearly.

## 9. Reporting
- Run daily.
- Notify only on new A+ or A roles. Otherwise say there are no new qualifying opportunities.
- Highlight deadlines, unusually strong matches, and new openings at top employers.
- Keep one Google Sheets master tracker.

## 10. Philosophy
Optimize for the intersection of realistic fit, strong pay, remote flexibility,
employer quality and future-facing creative leadership. The ideal role leads creative
strategy and production while introducing AI systems that make the team faster,
more consistent and more effective.
